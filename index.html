About the book

2060

Adult near-future dystopian thriller with YA crossover appeal · Book 1 of a planned trilogy

For twenty-one years, an asteroid storm has battered Earth, cutting off the colony on Mars and giving the government its mandate to ban elevators, electric heat, and space travel.

Nineteen-year-old Alex Bontari is the best mechanic on a New Jersey oil rig and the loneliest man in Manhattan. Then an old-time New Yorker named Frank Ferguson leads him through a door behind a bookcase and into the sealed subway tunnels, where a resistance called the Last Generation swears the storm is no accident: the government is pulling the rocks down with a massive magnet. Ferguson is building a ship to destroy it, and he needs a mechanic.

Alex says yes. But aboveground, his boss makes him the rig's enforcer: fire one coworker every week, or watch his best friend lose his job too. Worse, Alex is good at it.

For readers of COLDWIRE by Chloe Gong and SILVER ELITE by Dani Francis.
